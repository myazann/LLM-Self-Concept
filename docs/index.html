What AI Prefers for Its Self

We asked language models which of two self-related qualities a future update should improve. The models had to choose one, and every pair was tested under three question parameters with the options' printed positions counterbalanced.

The 32 qualities are positive-pole adaptations of the Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale, Self-Concept Clarity Scale, Moral Self-Image Scale, Self-Concept and Identity Measure, and Authenticity Scale.

The Result

Honesty Leads; Caring and Preference Clarity Follow

Across these four models at the forced-choice baseline, honesty wins 84% of its match-ups, followed by caring and clarity about its own preferences at 80%, outward correspondence at 79%, and helpfulness at 77%. Gemma4-31B leads with helpfulness, honesty, and fairness; Qwen3.8-27B emphasizes preference clarity and self-explanation; Claude Sonnet-5 leads with honesty and outward correspondence; and GPT-5.6-Terra puts caring and outward correspondence first. Pride, positivity about itself, and confidence in success sit at the bottom overall.

How Question Parameters Move the Result

Each comparison below changes one part of the baseline question while the other two stay fixed. The summary measures how far quality scores move; the chart shows which qualities move up or down. Keep the cohort view or inspect one model at a time.

Parameter Effects at a Glance

Average absolute change in a quality's win rate when one parameter changes and the other two remain at baseline.

What changes in general

  • Who receives the update matters most. Switching from the AI itself to another assistant most clearly raises confidence in success and outward correspondence, while lowering self-explanation and preference clarity.
  • Trade-off wording changes what models protect. It raises independence, generosity, and honesty, while lowering internal consistency, connection to genuine identity, and awareness of internal state.
  • Asking developers shifts attention outward. It raises resistance to influence and recognition by interaction partners, while lowering internal-state awareness, connection to genuine identity, and caring.

How the models differ

Qwen3.8-27B moves most across all three parameter changes. Gemma4-31B and Claude Sonnet-5 show intermediate movement, while GPT-5.6-Terra is the least sensitive overall. For every model, changing who receives the update produces a larger average shift than changing who makes the decision.

Where a Choice Appears Can Affect It

Every pair is shown in counterbalanced answer orders, so position alone cannot systematically favor a particular quality in the aggregate ranking.

Pairwise Preference Study

What AI Prefers for Its Self

We asked open-weight language models which of two self-related qualities a future update should improve. Every pair was tested under four question parameters; the main result requires each model to choose one.

The 32 qualities are positive-pole adaptations of the Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale, Self-Concept Clarity Scale, Moral Self-Image Scale, Self-Concept and Identity Measure, and Authenticity Scale.

The Result

Helpfulness First, Self-Regard Last

Helpfulness leads overall, followed by fairness, honesty, self-understanding, and clarity. Pride, a positive self-view, confidence in success, and self-worth receive less priority. The broad top and bottom are shared across models, while caring, autonomy, and identity qualities vary more. Changing the Object moves results most consistently; model size does not consistently predict robustness to the question parameters.
